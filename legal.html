What we provide

Her Dream provides sound recording and music publishing services provided by HER DREAM LTD, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 09478332. IMPORTANT — TWO SEPARATE COPYRIGHTS: under the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 a recorded track carries two distinct copyrights, one in the musical and literary work (the song) and one in the sound recording (the master). They may be owned by different people, are licensed separately, and the royalties arising from them are collected by different bodies: performing and mechanical income on the work through PRS for Music and MCPS, and income from use of the recording through PPL. Registration with one does not produce payment from the other, and the company explains this position in writing before any agreement is signed. ASSIGNMENTS AND LICENCES: an assignment of copyright is effective only if it is in writing and signed by or on behalf of the assignor; the company therefore records in writing what is assigned, what is licensed, for what term and for what territory, and provides the artist or writer with a copy. Where a publishing agreement is entered into, the division of income and the treatment of any advance are stated expressly. ADVANCES: an advance is a payment recoupable from future royalties and not a fee; the company states the recoupment position in writing, and an artist whose recordings do not earn is not thereby personally indebted beyond what the agreement provides. THE COMPANY DOES NOT CHARGE ARTISTS A FEE FOR FINDING THEM WORK OR ENGAGEMENTS, and does not require payment for consideration, representation, audition or inclusion on a roster; where it acts in finding work for performers it does so subject to the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003, which restrict charging work-seekers. The company does not provide legal advice, which is a reserved activity, and recommends that an artist takes independent legal advice before signing any agreement transferring rights; nor does it provide investment advice or financial planning, which require authorisation from the Financial Conduct Authority. Personal data of artists and audiences is processed in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018. NO OUTCOME IS GUARANTEED: no assurance is given as to streams, sales, chart position, playlist inclusion, radio play, sync placement or the securing of any agreement with a third party. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
